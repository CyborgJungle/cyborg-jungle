  Here the signal arrives at a wedding and refuses to leave. Diamonds open over
  faces, circles drift across a bride and her guests, and every frame becomes a
  negotiation between the moment as it was lived and the moment as the machine
  prefers to store it.


  Subject 506550 is the largest record from the archive's first winter, and its
  most crowded. It raises a question the single portraits avoid: when an entire
  gathering of people is converted into current, does their togetherness survive
  the conversion, or does each of them arrive alone?
