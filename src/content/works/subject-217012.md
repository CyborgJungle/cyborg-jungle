  Every window is a decision about what may be seen. The first is placed over
  his eyes; the others follow, cutting the face into slits, blocks and finally
  diamonds, until the man is assembled entirely out of openings.


  The early theorists of the electronic image believed the screen would be a
  window onto the world. Subject 217012 suggests something less comfortable:
  that the window was never transparent, and that whatever looks back through it
  has been arranged.
