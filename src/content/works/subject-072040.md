  In January the archive went quiet and grey. Figures appear only as negatives,
  as silhouettes against a mixer's channel readout, as fragments of a face
  caught in the edge of another signal. Between them, the image sometimes gives
  up on people altogether and turns to liquid.


  Subject 072040 documents the moments when the transmission seems unsure what
  it is carrying. A body, a pattern, a number on a display: to the tube they are
  all the same voltage. Only we insist on the difference.
